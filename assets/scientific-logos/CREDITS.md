# Scientific ecosystem logo sources

The README grid uses individual logo files from Pize's public website and the tools' official websites rather than a screenshot. The artwork is copied without alteration; sizing, labels, table backgrounds, and borders are handled by the README layout. Stata, Plotly, and Matplotlib use separate light and dark assets. Every logo and caption links to the corresponding tool's official website.

Names and logos belong to their respective owners. The grid includes the tools listed on the [Pize share page](https://pize.ai/zh-Hans/share), plus MATLAB, Matplotlib, ggplot2, Seaborn, ParaView, and Blender as additional scientific computing and visualization tools. Their inclusion does not establish a supported integration or imply endorsement or an official partnership; consult [Pize's documentation](https://pize.ai/docs) for product support. No new license to the third-party marks is granted by this repository.
